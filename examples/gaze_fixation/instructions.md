# Gaze fixation

The mouse cursor represents your gaze.

Move the cursor onto the central fixation point. Keep it inside the fixation
area until the trial finishes. If fixation is not acquired within 2 seconds,
or the cursor leaves during the hold, the trial will be tried again.

Press SPACE to begin. Press ESC during a trial to skip it, P to pause, or
Ctrl+C to end the session cleanly.
