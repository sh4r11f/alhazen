# Staircase detection

Watch the central fixation point. A target will briefly appear on either the
left or the right.

Press the LEFT ARROW if it appeared on the left, or the RIGHT ARROW if it
appeared on the right. Please respond even when the target is faint; the
staircases adjust its contrast based on your answers.

Press SPACE to begin. Press ESC during a trial to skip it, P to pause, or
Ctrl+C to end the session cleanly.
